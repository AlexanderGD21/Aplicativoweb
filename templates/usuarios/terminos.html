We may temporarily limit repeated sign-in or recovery attempts to protect accounts and service availability.

3. Corpus and educational content

Translations, categories, definitions, and pronunciations are provided for educational reference. The corpus is continuously reviewed and may contain regional variants, incomplete information, or errors.

Automated classification is not presented as a linguistic certification and does not replace the judgment of teachers, speakers, or specialist researchers.

5. Availability and corpus changes

The project may correct words, change categories, remove features, or temporarily interrupt the service for maintenance or security. It does not promise uninterrupted availability, universal synchronization, artificial-intelligence features, or services that are not visible in the application.

6. Account closure and changes

You can delete your account from profile options after confirming your password. We may also restrict an account in response to abuse, security risks, or a breach of these terms.

If these terms change materially, a new version and effective date will be published. Any future acceptance must be linked to the applicable version.
